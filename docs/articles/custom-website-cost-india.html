How Much Does a Custom Website Cost in India?

"How much will my website cost?" is the first question almost every business owner asks. There is no single flat price, because a website can be a simple one-page portfolio or a full online store. This guide explains what changes the price, so you can plan your budget without surprises.

Rough Price Ranges

Prices in India vary a lot from one team to another. These are rough ranges for small businesses and startups, not fixed rates:

  • Simple portfolio or small business site (1 to 5 pages): about ₹5,000 to ₹10,000.
  • Custom-designed site with animations: about ₹15,000 to ₹25,000.
  • E-commerce store with a backend: about ₹30,000 and up, depending on products and payment setup.
  • 3D or highly interactive website: usually higher, because it needs more design and testing time.

Treat these as a starting point. The final price depends on the five things below.

1. The Size and Type of Website

A simple informational site with Home, About, Services and Contact pages takes far less work than an online store with products, a cart and payments. More pages and more features mean more design and development time, and that is what you pay for.

2. Template or Custom Design

A template-based site is faster and cheaper, but many other sites look similar. A custom design is planned around your brand, so it takes longer and costs more, but it feels unique. Animations and 3D elements add to this, because they need extra design work and careful testing on mobile phones.

3. Features and Functionality

Every extra feature adds to the cost. Common ones are:

  • Contact forms with email inquiries or click-to-chat
  • Online booking or appointment forms
  • Login areas and dashboards
  • Payment gateways and product management
  • Basic SEO setup, such as page titles, descriptions and clean URLs

4. Content: Text, Logo and Images

Good content is part of a good website. If you already have your text, logo and photos ready, the project moves faster. If you need writing, logo design or photography, that is usually extra, so it is worth deciding early who will provide what.

5. Costs That Are Often Not Included

Some costs sit outside the design and development price, so ask about them before you start:

  • Domain name: renewed every year.
  • Hosting: some options are free for simple sites, others are paid monthly or yearly.
  • Paid plugins and tools: for example, email or payment services.
  • Maintenance: updates, fixes and new features after launch are usually charged separately.

How to Get a Fair Quote

Before you ask any team for a price, write down these points. It helps you compare quotes properly:

  • What your website needs to do (show your work, sell products, collect inquiries)
  • How many pages you expect
  • Whether you already have text, logo and images
  • Your budget range and your deadline

Ask for the quote in writing, with a clear list of what is included and what is not. A very low price with an unclear scope often turns into extra charges later.
