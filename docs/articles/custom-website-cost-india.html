How Much Does a Custom Website Cost in India?

One of the first questions every business owner asks is: "What is this going to cost me?" The truth is, there is no single flat price for a website. A custom website is an investment, and the cost depends entirely on what you need it to do.

1. The Type of Website

A simple 5-page informational site is very different from a complex e-commerce store.

  • Basic Business Website: Usually ranges from ₹15,000 to ₹40,000. This covers a clean design, mobile responsiveness, and standard pages (Home, About, Services, Contact).
  • E-Commerce Store: Can range from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000+. You are paying for payment gateway integrations, product management, and security.
  • Custom Web App / 3D Website: ₹1,00,000 and up. This requires complex coding, database management, and heavy design work.

2. Design Complexity

Do you want a standard template, or a unique brand experience? A custom UI/UX design takes more time. At returnXERO, we specialize in immersive 2D and 3D designs. Building 3D elements requires specialized developers, which increases the investment but creates a much higher impact.

3. Features and Functionality

The more features you add, the higher the cost. Here is what drives up the price:

  • Custom booking systems (for salons or clinics)
  • User login portals and dashboards
  • Multi-language support
  • Advanced SEO setup and speed optimization

4. Ongoing Maintenance

A website is not a "set it and forget it" tool. You need to pay for domain renewals, hosting, and security updates. Many agencies charge a monthly retainer for maintenance, while custom developers might charge per update.
